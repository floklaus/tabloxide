Colour themes

The view standards apply to each theme: Light, Dark, Neon and High contrast. System uses Light or Dark. A theme changes colours only. This part gives the colours for each theme and the rules that keep the states clear.

11. What stays the same in each theme

  • Anatomy, sizes, density and the 4 px grid (part 1, sections 1 and 2).
  • Type roles and the monospace rule (part 1, section 3).
  • Status shapes and their order (part 1, section 4).
  • Keys, focus order and the empty, loading and error states (part 2).
  • Icons: one set, stroke 1.75. Only their colour comes from the theme.

14. Rules for each theme

All themes

  • The shape tells the state. The colour helps. A theme may change a colour, never a shape.
  • Warning and the accent must look different. The accent marks the active tool and AI actions only.
  • Each status mark reaches 3:1 on the panel and on the selected row. Text reaches 4.5:1.
  • The selected row keeps its text weight. The focus ring is drawn on top of the selection.

Light and Dark

  • Warning moves to a yellow brown (#a16207) in Light and to yellow (#facc15) in Dark, away from the amber accent.
  • The environment badges keep their colours.
  • Dark selection: #1e3a5f, with the normal text colour.

Neon

  • Error moves to a neon orange red (#ff6b3d), away from the magenta accent and the magenta SQL keywords.
  • Busy and the focus ring are both cyan. The arc and the ring around the row keep them apart.
  • The primary button is cyan with dark text (#0d0221).

High contrast

  • Selected row: white fill, black text, black status mark.
  • Focus ring: 2 px yellow with a 2 px black gap, so it shows on black and on the white selection.
  • Warning, accent and the primary button are all yellow. So an AI action always shows its text label, not only the sparkle.
  • Environment badges and panels have a 1 px white border. Nothing relies on a fill colour alone.
