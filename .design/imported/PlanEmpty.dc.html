1. The statement at the cursor reads data

Explain the statement at the cursor

items.sql · lines 1 to 3 · Local shop 
select * from item
where price > 10
order by created_at desc;
  • Explain shows the estimate of the planner. The database does not run the statement.
  • Explain and measure runs the statement and measures each step. A confirmation comes first.

2. The statement at the cursor changes data

delete from item where price < 1;
The statement changes data. Explain and measure is not available.

With no statement at the cursor, the tab says "Put the cursor in a statement." and the two buttons are not available.
